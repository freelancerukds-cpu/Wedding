Our Story & Heritage

The Lumina Frames Journey

Founded in 2018 in the heart of T. Nagar, Chennai, Lumina Frames & Events began with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between cinematic storytelling and meticulous event execution.

What started as a passionate two-person boutique studio capturing intimate family celebrations has grown into one of Chennai's premier visual production and full-service event curation houses. Our founders recognized that clients did not just want photographs or venue bookings—they wanted cohesive, stress-free narratives where every single frame and minute detail resonated with authenticity and luxury.

Our Core Philosophy
